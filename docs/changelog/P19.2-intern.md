# P19.2 (intern side): mobile fixes after the Dada rebrand and the questions inbox

Audit of the intern journeys (chat, «أسئلتك لمشرفك», Rumour Check, mentor requests, plan, progress, quiz,
scenarios, About) at 360, 390, 414, 768 and 1280 px, Arabic and English, against a local offline server. No page
scrolled sideways at any width; the fixes below are layout and tap-target fixes, with no behaviour change.

- Chat dock bars («من مشرفك», «أسئلتك لمشرفك»): the visible "Show/Hide requests|questions" label is now screen-reader
  only at every width (it was hidden on phones only). At 768 and 1280 px the label pushed the titles to "From your …"
  / «من م…» and, beside the recall banner, ran «Your mentor answered your question» under the label (overlapping
  text). Titles wrap instead of being cut; the bars and the recall banner take 15rem / 16rem before sharing a row.
- Chat dock while typing on a short screen (keyboard open in a browser that resizes the page, or a phone held
  sideways, max-height 32rem): the strip of bars folds away while the composer has focus, so the thread keeps
  about 240 px instead of 80 px at 360 x 440. It comes back on blur; focusing a bar is not typing.
- «أرسل إلى مشرفك» sheet: Send and Cancel stick to the sheet's bottom edge, so Send stays in view when the
  keyboard makes the sheet short (it was below the fold at 360 x 440 in English).
- 44 px targets: the practice bar's «إنهاء التدريب» (was 40), the composer text box (40 to 44, same box height),
  the request status tiles (40 px from 640 px up, «تم» 38 px wide at 768) and the reply thread's attach, send,
  remove-file and file-download buttons (36 px from 640 px up) now keep 44 px up to 1024 px.
- Tests: chatux (bars show a chevron, the strip folds only while the composer has focus), questions (sheet actions
  sticky; the bar's label stays its accessible name), requests (status tile size).
