### P14.3 — the customer pushes back «الزبون بيضغط عليك»  (2026-11-12)
- What now exists:
  - `data/ours/scenario_customer_lines.csv` (`scenario_id, customer_ar, source`): one authored Jordanian-Arabic
    pressure line per scenario S01–S06, pushing the trainee to promise or confirm what they cannot know
    (S02 «أكيد؟ رح أشتريها عشانها بالذات…», S04 asks for the exact arrival without naming a time). No number,
    name, time, honorific, contact or policy noun; every line passes `verify_small_talk`.
  - `app/scenarios.py`: `load_customer_lines(ours_dir)` (absent file → no lines), `Scenario.customer_ar`,
    `ScenarioIndex(..., customer_lines=None)`; every `Feedback` (option, offline free text, LLM checklist)
    carries the scenario's line. Identical in both modes; no line-picking logic.
  - Web: `customerTurnIds(items)` in `chat/model.ts` marks the first `scenario_feedback` after each
    `scenario_start` of the same scenario. That card shows a customer bubble above the notes, a prompt to reply,
    and folds the suggested response behind «علّقت؟» (`aria-expanded`). Later feedback in the run, and any
    feedback without `customer_ar`, renders the normal card. Works for practice started in chat and from the
    scenarios screen (`#/chat?scenario=`), which hands off to the same thread.
- Interfaces added/changed: `Feedback.customer_ar: str | None = None` (schemas, `types.ts` optional field and
  guard, the three feedback fixtures, `INDEX.md`, `api_contract.md`). i18n block `pushback.*` (ar + en).
- Acceptance: `tests/test_customer_lines.py` (format, 6 lines, Arabic only, ≤ 160 chars, small-talk checks, every
  feedback carries the line, no digit); `test_contract.py::test_feedback_customer_line_is_optional`;
